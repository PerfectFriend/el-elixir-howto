## 1. Theory

### General Product Description
El Elixir is a high-quality natural vegetable oil blend based on cannabis extract. It is intended for internal consumption and external application (skin, hair, nails). Benefits are claimed for people of almost any age, as well as for pets (due to the similarity of the endocannabinoid system in mammals).

The product is positioned as a “magical” way to support the body: CBD relieves symptoms, THC “switches on the regeneration mode” of the immune system, the central nervous system as a whole, and subsequent healing of the body. The fatty base of coconut oil makes it an excellent solvent that penetrates tissues well, while the essential hemp seed oils serve as an excellent nutritional base for tissue regeneration.

### Mechanism of Action (according to the materials)
- **Apoptosis of damaged cells.** The Elixir is said to “switch on” apoptosis inside overly damaged and mutated cells, after which they quickly perish. In severe cases (for example, after radiation burns in terminal-stage cancer patients with a destroyed immune system) this can create a critical load on the body — additional consultation is required.
- **The immune system as “personal magic”.** A human is described as a biorobot working on electricity (glucose → ATP → electrical impulses of neurons). The immune system is the main tool of recovery: destruction of pathogenic/mutated cells and rapid growth of new tissues.
- **Role of THC and CBD (1:1).**  
  - CBD — symptomatic relief.  
  - THC — activation of regeneration of the immune system and CNS.  
- **Necessary conditions for the “magic”:**  
  - Sufficient “building materials” (proteins, oils).  
  - Long sleep (bed rest).  
  - Walks in fresh air and sun (morning and sunset).  
  - Special diet (see below).

### Diet and Regime Required for the Effect
- Hemp seeds: 1 g per 1 kg of body weight per day.  
- Kefir from whole milk — 1 litre per day (fresh every day).  
- A piece of butter daily (catalyst).  
- Minimum carbohydrates and sugar.  
- In the morning — porridge (buckwheat/oats) with butter and elixir + fruits (apples, bananas, pineapple).  
- Walks: 2 hours a day in the sun (one hour in the morning + one hour at sunset).  
- Maximum bed rest and sleep — “this is resurrection and the work of the immune system”.

### Indications (list from the materials)
Insomnia, joint and muscle pains and cramps, arthritis, Alzheimer’s, gout, skin lesions, joint and vertebral pains, anemia, glaucoma, migraine, loss of appetite, nausea, Parkinson’s, cancer, tuberculosis, viral infections, inflammation, skin diseases — psoriasis, ulcers, papillomas, herpes, mental disorders, etc.  
Separately noted is the aphrodisiac effect and regulation of the menstrual cycle in women 50+.

### Warnings
- **Children:** it is currently **HIGHLY recommended to KEEP FROM CHILDREN**. Possible protocols for children — in the future.  
- **Pets:** can be given in drops to make them behave more calmly and friendly.  
- **External application:** a test is mandatory on a small skin area (5×5 mm).  
- **Internal use:** start with a minimal dose (1 drop / “dot”), increase gradually while observing the reaction.  
- In terminal severe cases a critical reaction of the body is possible due to mass apoptosis.

### Dosing (according to the materials)
- Strong starting guideline: ≈ 1 mg per 10 kg of body mass (for those who have never encountered this kind of product before).  
- Recommended sensitivity testing scheme:  
  1. Start with 1 drop.  
  2. Wait 3–4 hours.  
  3. If no effect — double it.  
  4. Wait 4–5 hours.  
  5. Repeat doubling until a noticeable effect appears.  
- Main sensations at the working dose: deep relaxation, hunger, weakness, desire to sleep.

## 3. Descriptions of Healing Cases (anecdotal stories from the materials)

All stories below are given according to the meaning of the provided text. These are personal accounts of the author/representatives of the product. Official medicine does not confirm these cases; they are of a testimonial nature.

### Case 1. Terminal cancer patient (“Panda” / Seva) in Israel
The patient arrived in Israel after courses of chemo- and radiotherapy in the state of a “bald skeleton covered with scabs instead of skin”, weight ≈ 35 kg, on the verge of death.  
A course of El Elixir was prepared according to the “secret recipe”. Regime: elixir internally + externally on the scabs, long sleep, walks in fresh air, healthy food.  
After ≈ 20 days: scabs fell off, new skin grew, hair began to grow, 20 kg of mass was gained. The patient “changed his mind about dying”. Later a romantic relationship developed with the person who cared for him.  
The story is presented as “almost a resurrection” and direct proof of the effectiveness of the recipe brought from India.

### Case 2. Cancer remission (message in correspondence)
The patient took the concentrate/elixir for about a month. Tests (the last ones a week before the message) showed that “cancer was defeated”, remission occurred. The patient feels well. The author of the correspondence expresses gratitude.

### Case 3. HIV in a 12-year-old child (adopted nephew)
The child was infected with HIV in an orphanage. After moving to Spain, a medical examination detected 800 units of the virus.  
In parallel with the prescribed drug Triumeq, the child was given previously prepared El Elixir. Bed rest was observed for ≈ 3 weeks (the child slept a lot and complained of weakness).  
Repeat analysis: the virus was not detected (“it became zero”). The analysis was redone twice — the result was confirmed. The author of the story considers the mission accomplished; later the child studied and even got married.

### Case 4. Terminal cancer of a friend’s mother from Ukraine
The friend’s mother was told after the last attempt of radio- and chemotherapy to “get used to the earth”. The author smuggled 350 g of THC. From half of it an elixir was made almost on pure THC (CBD is an error). The second half was sent to “the guys on the front line”.  
The further development of events is presented in the text as “the most interesting part”, but the specific outcome is not detailed in the provided materials (it is suggested to “show a reaction” for the continuation of the story).

### Additional Notes from the Materials
- “Bad stories” are mentioned when people started too late and died anyway.  
- Separately noted is the effect of menstrual cycle regulation in women over 50.  
- The author repeatedly emphasises that the recipe “absolutely works in a wide range of the most terrible problems” and was brought from India.
