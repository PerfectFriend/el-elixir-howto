## 1. Theory

### General Product Description
El Elixir is a high-quality natural vegetable oil blend based on cannabis extract. It is intended for internal consumption and external application (skin, hair, nails). It has been used by people of various ages as well as by pets (due to the similarity of the endocannabinoid system in mammals).

The product is used as a means of supporting the body: CBD relieves symptoms, THC activates regenerative processes of the immune system, the central nervous system, and subsequent tissue recovery. The fatty base of coconut oil provides good tissue penetration; hemp oil serves as a nutritional base for regeneration.

### Mechanism of Action
- **Apoptosis of damaged cells.** The composition contributes to the initiation of apoptosis in severely damaged and mutated cells. In severe cases (for example, after radiation burns in terminal-stage cancer patients with a heavily compromised immune system) a significant load on the body is possible — caution and monitoring are required.
- **Immune system.** The body is viewed as a system operating on cellular energy supply (glucose → ATP). The immune system performs the functions of clearing pathogenic and mutated cells and restoring tissues.
- **Role of THC and CBD (1:1).**  
  - CBD — symptomatic relief.  
  - THC — activation of regenerative processes of the immune system and CNS.  
- **Necessary conditions:**  
  - Sufficient proteins and oils.  
  - Prolonged sleep / bed rest.  
  - Walks in fresh air and sun (morning and sunset).  
  - Special diet.

### Diet and Regime
- Hemp seeds: 1 g per 1 kg of body weight per day.  
- Kefir from whole milk — 1 litre per day (fresh every day).  
- A piece of butter daily.  
- Minimum carbohydrates and sugar.  
- In the morning — porridge (buckwheat / oats) with butter and elixir + fruits (apples, bananas, pineapple).  
- Walks: 2 hours a day in the sun (one hour in the morning + one hour at sunset).  
- Maximum bed rest and sleep.

### Indications (based on experience of use)
Insomnia, joint and muscle pains and cramps, arthritis, Alzheimer’s, gout, skin lesions, joint and vertebral pains, anemia, glaucoma, migraine, loss of appetite, nausea, Parkinson’s, cancer, tuberculosis, viral infections, inflammation, psoriasis, ulcers, papillomas, herpes, mental disorders, etc.  
Influence on the menstrual cycle in women 50+ and an aphrodisiac effect have also been noted.

### Warnings
- **Children:** the product must be kept out of reach of children.  
- **Pets:** application in drops is possible.  
- **External application:** a preliminary test on a small skin area (5×5 mm) is mandatory.  
- **Internal use:** start with a minimal dose (1 drop), increase gradually while observing the reaction.  
- In severe terminal cases a strong reaction of the body is possible.

### Dosing
- Strong starting guideline: ≈ 1 mg per 10 kg of body mass (for those who have not previously used similar compositions).  
- Dose titration scheme:  
  1. Start with 1 drop.  
  2. Wait 3–4 hours.  
  3. If no effect — double it.  
  4. Wait 4–5 hours.  
  5. Repeat doubling until a noticeable effect appears.  
- Main sensations at the working dose: deep relaxation, hunger, weakness, sleepiness.
